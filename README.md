# Gracelynd & Company — Public Case Studies

This repository contains sanitized public case studies, architectures, frameworks, and proof-of-work from Gracelynd & Company.

The portfolio focuses on **Capacity Recovery**: identifying repetitive operational work, separating repeatable processing from human judgment, applying the appropriate automation or AI capability, and returning human capacity to higher-value work.

**Discover → Measure → Separate → Automate → Recover**

> Technology handles repeatability. People handle judgment. The organization recovers capacity.

## Purpose

This repository serves two audiences:

- **Organizations and prospective clients** can inspect the methods, architecture, controls, and outcomes behind Gracelynd's work.
- **Technical and professional reviewers** can inspect documented proof-of-work demonstrating automation design, operational transformation, governance, implementation thinking, and measurable outcomes.

## Public-safety model

This repository is a publication destination, not Gracelynd's internal source of truth. Working files, internal evidence, credentials, client-confidential material, identifying information, and non-public implementation details remain in private governed systems.

Only artifacts that pass an explicit public-safety and publication review are promoted here. Public case studies may use generalized organization descriptions, sanitized examples, reconstructed diagrams, or representative data where needed to protect confidentiality.

## Case studies

Public case studies will be maintained under `case-studies/` as they are approved for release.

Each package may include:

- business problem and measurable outcome;
- Capacity Recovery analysis;
- architecture and workflow documentation;
- human/automation boundary;
- technology decisions;
- sanitized implementation examples;
- lessons learned and controls; and
- a link to the canonical Gracelynd.com case study.

## Frameworks

Reusable public methods and patterns will be maintained under `frameworks/`, including the Capacity Recovery framework as it develops.

## Disclosure

Case studies describe work accurately while protecting confidential and identifying information. Where work was developed outside a Gracelynd client engagement, the relevant case study states that relationship explicitly and does not imply client status or endorsement.
